# 135-onboard-star-battle

Onboard the **star-battle** family. It generates **nothing** until every item below exists and is
committed — this is the FAMILY ONBOARDING CONTRACT in `CLAUDE.md`, and it is the reason the
guarantee holds across families rather than only for the first one.

1. `solver/star-battle/` — the independent solver, with a technique ladder and a documented
   canonical encoding and symmetry group.
2. Reference support for star-battle in `solver/reference/` — deliberately dumb, obviously correct,
   sharing no code with the solver.
3. `fixtures/star-battle/` — at least 40 fixtures with known verdicts, covering unique, multiple,
   unsolvable, near-misses and degenerate shapes. Sources in `fixtures/star-battle/SOURCES.md`.
4. A green `node tools/harden.mjs star-battle --write-report`, with `HARDENING/star-battle.md`
   committed.
5. `solver/star-battle/bands.json` — calibrated once against the fixtures and frozen.
6. `site/play/star-battle.html` — a working player passing at least five of its own selftests,
   self-contained and readable at 360px.
7. `corpus/star-battle/family.json` — regenerated by `tools/build_index.mjs`.

**This plan has failed if** any puzzle of this family is generated before all seven exist, or if
the hardening report is not green.
