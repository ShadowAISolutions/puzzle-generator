The guarantee

This puzzle is machine-generated. An independent solver, which shares no code with the generator that made it, proved that it has exactly one solution by counting solutions exhaustively and stopping at two. A separate brute-force reference confirmed that verdict.

Its difficulty band is measured, not asserted: it is the highest technique tier the easiest complete solution path needs. Bands 1 to 4 are solvable by pure deduction with no guessing. Band 5 is the only band where bounded search is required, and it is always flagged.

Your solution is checked here in your browser, against the record. Nothing is sent anywhere.
